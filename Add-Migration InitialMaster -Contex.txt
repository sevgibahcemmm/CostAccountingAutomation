1. Master migration'ı

Add-Migration InitialMaster -Context MasterDbContext -OutputDir Migrations/Master -Project Cost.Accounting.Automation.Infrastructure -StartupProject Cost.Accounting.Automation.WinFormsApp


2. Yıl migration'ı (önce ortam değişkenini ayarlayın)

$env:CAA_ALLOW_YEAR_DESIGN_TIME = "1"
Add-Migration InitialYear -Context ApplicationDbContext -OutputDir Migrations/Year -Project Cost.Accounting.Automation.Infrastructure -StartupProject Cost.Accounting.Automation.WinFormsApp


3. Ortam değişkenini kaldırın
Remove-Item Env:CAA_ALLOW_YEAR_DESIGN_TIME



4. Kontrol

Add-Migration CheckMaster -Context MasterDbContext -Project Cost.Accounting.Automation.Infrastructure -StartupProject Cost.Accounting.Automation.WinFormsApp


  Remove-Migration -Context MasterDbContext -Project Cost.Accounting.Automation.Infrastructure -StartupProject Cost.Accounting.Automation.WinFormsApp
